# Classroom Capacity

Live classes run in three regions; all times are UTC. Asia-Pacific classes fill the early hours, Europe the morning and
the Americas the afternoon and evening. The weekly Wednesday webinar and the Sunday-night homework deadline bring the
biggest crowds, so they get the most capacity.
